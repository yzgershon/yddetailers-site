Booking and payment

  • You can book online at yddetailers.com or by phone/text at (248) 346-5194.
  • Prices shown online are starting prices based on your vehicle size and the package and add-ons you pick. If your vehicle needs significantly more work than described (for example, heavy pet hair, mold, or biohazards not mentioned at booking), we'll tell you before starting and agree on any price change together.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • You can cancel or reschedule for free up to 12 hours before your appointment. Just call or text us.

Weather

If it rains and you have a garage or covered space, we can usually still do the detail. If not, we'll reach out and reschedule you to the next available day at no extra charge.

What we need from you

  • A parking spot where we can safely work on your vehicle.

Vehicle condition and liability

  • We treat every car like our own and use products appropriate for your vehicle's surfaces.

Satisfaction

If something isn't up to standard, tell us before we leave or within 24 hours and we'll fix it. That's the deal behind our money-back guarantee.
