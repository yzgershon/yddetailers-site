03 · Our Story

Two guys.
One obsession.

YD Detailers started as a weekend hustle between two friends who couldn’t stand a dirty car. We bring the rig to your driveway, plug into your hose, and treat your car the way we treat ours — patiently, by hand, with chemistry that respects the paint.

No upselling. No fluorescent lobby. Just two clean cars after we leave — yours, and the one we drove in on.

Beyond the cars, we care about the community we grew up in. In 2026, we helped raise over $500 for Newton North High School’s Business Club — because the same community that supports us deserves our support back.

— Yishai & Dan
