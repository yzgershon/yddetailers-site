Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

YDdetailers ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile car detailing service based in Newton, Massachusetts, serving Newton, Waltham, Watertown, Brookline, and the surrounding area. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a form to us, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and provide our service:

  • Contact details you give us — your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Booking and vehicle details — the service you're interested in, your vehicle type, and your location, so we can quote and schedule.
  • Information from ads and forms — if you reply to an ad on Facebook or Instagram, those platforms share the contact details you submit in their lead form with us.
  • Usage data — basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use our website (see "Cookies and tracking" below).

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote, booking, or question.
  • To schedule and provide your detailing service.
  • To follow up about your appointment or future service.
  • To improve our website and marketing.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us operate, such as our website host, our online booking tool, and analytics and advertising platforms (Google and Meta). These providers handle your data only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand site traffic and measure our advertising. These tools may set cookies and collect anonymized usage data. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalized ads through your Google and Meta account settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Just reach out using the contact info below and we'll take care of it.

Children's privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Contact YDdetailers at (248) 346-5194 or through yddetailers.com.
